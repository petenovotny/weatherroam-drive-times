# Data licences

## The drive-time table (`drive-table-*.bin`)

The table is a Derivative Database of OpenStreetMap and is made available
under the [Open Database Licence 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)
(ODbL). You may copy, distribute and adapt it, as long as you attribute
OpenStreetMap and share any adapted database under the same licence.

Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors. See
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

The table contains only integer GeoNames ids and drive times. It contains no
place names, coordinates or other GeoNames content.

## Origins and destinations (`origins-*.tsv`, `destinations-*.tsv`)

These lists are derived from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) and are
available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Attribution: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/.

## How they fit together

The table and the town lists are separate databases, joined only by GeoNames
id. Following the OpenStreetMap Foundation's
[Collective Database guideline](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Community_Guidelines/Collective_Database_Guideline_Guideline),
the ODbL share-alike terms apply to the table, and the GeoNames lists keep
their own licence.

This is how we read the licences, not legal advice.
